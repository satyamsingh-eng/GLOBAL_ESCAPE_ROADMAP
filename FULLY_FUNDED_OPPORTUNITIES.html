🇩🇪 DAAD WISE (Working Internships in Science and Engineering)

Any publicly funded German Higher Education Institution or National Research Institute (Max Planck, Helmholtz, Fraunhofer, Leibniz)
Location

Pan-Germany (Chosen by applicant and host professor)

Duration

2 to 3 months (Between May and August)

Deadline (2025/2026)

November 1 – November 10 annually (Portal opens in October; cold emailing German professors recommended in August–September)

Funding Package
✅ Flight ❌ Housing

€831 – €936/month living stipend (€25/day) + €1,050 one-time lump-sum travel allowance + full DAAD health/accident/liability insurance

🟡 Eligibility Details Required

Conditional / Gated fit. Hard eligibility rules: (1) Restricted to applicants enrolled at designated DAAD partner Indian institutions (IITs, NITs, IIITs, BITS, and select AICTE/NAAC tier-1 colleges); (2) Minimum aggregate CGPA ≥ 8.5/10 or 85%; (3) Must be in 5th/6th semester at the time of application and return to home university after the internship (graduating final-semester students are ineligible); (4) Requires obtaining an acceptance/invitation letter from a German professor before applying.
